\section*{The Arabic Definite Article}

The Arabic definite article is \textbf{alif-lam} (\arb{الـ}), which is the exact equivalent of the English word ``the''. 

\subsection*{Key Characteristics:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Always attached directly} to the noun (it is never written as a separate word).
    \item \textbf{Invariable}: It never changes its form based on the gender or number of the noun it modifies.
\end{itemize}

The 28 letters of the Arabic alphabet are divided evenly into two groups of 14 letters each based on how they phonetically interact with the definite article: \textbf{Moon Letters} (Non-Assimilating) and \textbf{Sun Letters} (Assimilating).
